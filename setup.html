Turn on the dyslexic font

Settings → Appearance → Chat font → Dyslexic Friendly. Built in, not an extension. Ten seconds.

2

Make one Project

Call it your job title. Paste your standing brief into the Project instructions. Every chat inside it starts with your context already loaded.

3

Set Sonnet 5 as your habit

Not a setting — a habit. Switch up to Opus deliberately, not by default.

Free feeds — tenders come to you

4

Public Contracts Scotland

Free supplier registration, then set an Alert Profile by commodity category and geography. Below-threshold notices appear only here — Find a Tender won't show them.

5

Find a Tender

Above-threshold UK-wide notices. Register separately. Between the two you stop hunting.

6

Two free horizon-scanning bulletins

Several major law firms publish free ESG horizon-scanning updates — lawyer-checked signal rather than raw search results. Subscribe to two, not ten.

gov.scot — consultations and policy.

The rule

  • AI tells you where to look and what it might mean
  • These sites tell you what it actually says
  • Anything going to a client or board gets checked against the source
