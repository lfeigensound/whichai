The standard job

Most real work is both. Always in this order.

1

Google gathers

Deep Research, or Notebook if it's your own files. Get the raw material and the citations.

2

Save it

Into Drive. Everyone skips this step, and it's why the work gets done twice.

3

Claude judges

Paste the findings in. What does it mean, what applies, what's missing, what's the risk.

4

Claude drafts

From the checked findings — never from memory. Memory is where inventions come from.

Warning signs

Gemini is drifting

STOP AND RESTART
  • "Great question! Let's unpack that"
  • Agreeing with something you know is wrong
  • Answering a question you didn't ask
  • Editing or creating files unprompted

Claude is drifting

STOP AND RESTART
  • Hedging on everything, committing to nothing
  • Confident claims with no source named
  • Longer answers that say less
  • Folding after you pushed back once

Both: when it drifts, start a new chat. Arguing it back on track costs more time than restarting.
