Which Claude model

Sonnet 5 — your default

Use it unless you have a reason not to. Fast, huge memory, handles almost everything.

Opus 5 — the hard ones

Complex analysis. Cross-referencing regulations. When being wrong would cost you.

Haiku 4.5 — quick and small

Reformatting, tidying, short lookups. Don't give it anything that needs judgement.

Before you send it anywhere

Check these yourself. Always.

  • "Is it still in force?" — open legislation.gov.uk yourself
